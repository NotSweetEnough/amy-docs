# Privacy statement

Amy AI ("Amy") is published by Valor Consulting Emanuel-Eduard Chirileanu ("we"). This statement explains what happens to data when you use Amy.

## We receive no data

- We operate **no backend, no AI service and no data store** for Amy.
- Amy contains **no telemetry, analytics or tracking**. We receive no Wiki, work-item, pull-request, usage or personal data from Amy.
- The Visual Studio Marketplace, operated by Microsoft, may share install statistics with publishers under its own terms. Microsoft's privacy statement applies to the Marketplace and to Azure DevOps.

## Where your data goes

- **Azure DevOps.** Amy runs in your browser inside Azure DevOps. It reads and writes Wiki pages and work items with your own permissions, only after you confirm each change.
- **Your organization's backend.** When you use an AI feature, Amy sends the content needed for it (for example the selected Wiki passages or work-item fields) to the backend that your organization's administrator configured. Your organization operates that backend and chooses its AI provider; their terms apply to that data, not ours.
- **Settings.** Amy stores the backend's address and display name, project templates and your confirmation of the backend in Azure DevOps extension data, within your organization. It never stores API keys or passwords there.

Details: [DATA-HANDLING.md](https://github.com/NotSweetEnough/amy-docs/blob/main/DATA-HANDLING.md).

## Support requests

If you contact us through the public issue tracker, what you write is public. Don't include personal data, credentials or content from your Azure DevOps organization.
